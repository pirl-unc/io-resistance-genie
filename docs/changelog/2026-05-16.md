## New findings

### 1. TREM2+ myeloid spatial exclusion co-defines CRC ICI resistance with multi-checkpoint T cell exhaustion

**Paper:** Mestrallet G, Samstein RM, Bhardwaj N. "Reprogramming T cell-myeloid crosstalk overcomes immune resistance in colorectal cancer." *Cell Reports Medicine* (2026-05-05). [DOI: 10.1016/j.xcrm.2026.102786](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2026.102786)

**Key finding:** Using orthotopic and patient-derived CRC models with single-cell and spatial transcriptomics, the authors identified dual mechanisms of ICI resistance: TREM2+ immunosuppressive macrophages spatially enriched in T cell-excluded niches, and multi-checkpoint-exhausted T cells (TIM3+LAG3+TIGIT+PD-1+). Spatial co-localization of CXCL9+MHC+C1Q+ macrophages with TCF+PRF1+ T cells predicts anti-PD-1 response. Quadruple blockade (PD-1 + CTLA-4 + LAG-3 + anti-TREM2) achieves 100% tumor clearance in dMMR CRC and >70% in MSS CRC orthotopic models — the first evidence that TREM2-mediated myeloid exclusion is co-tractable with conventional checkpoints in MSS CRC.

**Tier:** Emerging (preclinical only; no human clinical trial data yet)  
**Mechanism:** TME exclusion + T cell exhaustion  
**Pages updated:** `docs/index.md`, `docs/mechanisms/tme-exclusion.md`

### 2. Pan-cancer spatial ecotype taxonomy recoverable non-invasively from plasma cfDNA (Liquid EcoTyper)

**Paper:** Zhang W, Brown EL, Usmani A, Newman AM, Chaudhuri AA. "Non-invasive profiling of the tumour microenvironment with spatial ecotypes." *Nature* (2026-05-06). [DOI: 10.1038/s41586-026-10452-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10452-4)

**Key finding:** Machine-learning framework integrating 10M+ single-cell and spot-level spatial transcriptomes from diverse carcinomas and melanomas identifies 9 conserved spatial ecotypes (SEs) with unique biology, geospatial features, and clinical outcome associations — including several linked to immunotherapy response. SEs are recoverable from plasma cfDNA methylation via deep learning (Liquid EcoTyper). In ~100 melanoma patients on ICI, cfDNA-inferred SE levels show striking associations with response. First non-invasive reconstruction of tumor spatial TME state from blood.

**Tier:** Emerging (retrospective cfDNA cohort; prospective validation pending)  
**Mechanism:** TME exclusion / clinical biomarker  
**Pages updated:** `docs/index.md`, `docs/mechanisms/tme-exclusion.md`
