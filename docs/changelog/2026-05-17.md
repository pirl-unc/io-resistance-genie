## New findings

### 1. STING agonists paradoxically expand immunosuppressive Bregs via PI3Kγ in pancreatic cancer; dual SH-273/Nano-273 compound overcomes both arms of suppression

**Paper:** Li C, Mao S, Zhao H, et al. "Dual targeting of PI3Kγ and STING overcomes regulatory B cell- and myeloid cell-driven immune suppression in pancreatic cancer." *Nature Cancer* (2026-04-28). [DOI: 10.1038/s43018-026-01158-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43018-026-01158-7)

**Key finding:** STING agonists unexpectedly expand immunosuppressive regulatory B cells (Bregs) in PDAC via a PI3Kγ-dependent mechanism, partially offsetting STING's intended pro-inflammatory myeloid activation. The dual-targeting compound SH-273 and its albumin nanoformulation Nano-273 suppress STING-induced Breg expansion while sustaining IRF3-driven myeloid activation, achieving 201-day median survival in orthotopic pancreatic cancer models and reducing lung metastasis — outperforming STING agonist or PI3Kγ inhibitor monotherapy. Identifies regulatory B cells as a previously unrecognized TME exclusion-class resistance mechanism and explains mechanistically why STING monotherapy underperforms in an immunosuppressive TME.

**Classification:** Novel  
**Mechanism:** TME exclusion (Breg-mediated immune suppression)

### 2. KSR2 links Ras oncogenic signaling to anti-PD-1 resistance via Warburg-effect metabolic reprogramming

**Paper:** Ge Y, Zhou Q, Zhang Q. "KSR2 functions as a metabolic checkpoint for anti-PD-1 resistance by reprogramming glucose metabolism." *Cancer Immunology, Immunotherapy* (2026-04-21). [DOI: 10.1007/s00262-026-04394-z](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00262-026-04394-z)

**Key finding:** KSR2 (kinase suppressor of Ras 2), a Ras-RAF-MEK scaffold protein not previously connected to immune evasion, is upregulated in anti-PD-1-resistant lung cancer in mouse models and public clinical datasets. KSR2 overexpression confers resistance while knockdown resensitizes. Mechanistically, KSR2 drives Warburg-effect glucose reprogramming (enhanced uptake, lactate accumulation, disrupted TCA cycle), generating an immunosuppressive TME with reduced CD8+ T-cell infiltration and enriched Tregs. A conceptually novel connection between Ras scaffold oncogenesis and metabolic immune evasion as a druggable resistance axis.

**Classification:** Novel  
**Mechanism:** Metabolic (tumor-intrinsic metabolic reprogramming → immunosuppressive TME)  
**Pages updated:** `docs/index.md`, `docs/mechanisms/metabolic.md`
