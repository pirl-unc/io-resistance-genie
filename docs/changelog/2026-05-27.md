## Refined

### Microbiome taxa non-replication: melanoma-specific meta-analysis adds Faecalibacterium + SCFA signal

- **Classification:** refines
- **Paper:** Zhang X et al. "Meta-analytic microbiome target discovery for immune checkpoint inhibitor response in advanced melanoma." *Communications Medicine*, May 21, 2026. [DOI: 10.1038/s43856-026-01612-8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43856-026-01612-8)
- **What it adds:** Meta-analysis of 678 advanced melanoma patients across 7 ICI-treated cohorts finds that **Faecalibacterium abundance and SCFA (short-chain fatty acid) biosynthesis pathway enrichment** are the most reproducible cross-cohort microbiome features associated with ICI response in melanoma. This partially rehabilitates Faecalibacterium specifically within melanoma — which had been contested by the pan-cancer taxa non-replication literature — while reinforcing that functional pathway-level analysis is more cross-cohort reproducible than single-taxon abundance. Akkermansia remained non-monotonic even in this pooled dataset. **Refines** the contested taxa non-replication bullet in `docs/index.md` and `docs/mechanisms/microbiome.md` with a trailing clause.

## Confirmed (no text change)

### Pre-existing CD8+ TME infiltration predicts pembrolizumab benefit pan-cancer — confirmed in rare cancers basket trial

- **Classification:** confirms
- **Paper:** Naing A et al. "Tumor microenvironment features predict immunotherapy response across rare cancers: biomarker analysis from a phase 2 basket trial." *Cell Reports Medicine*, May 20, 2026. [DOI: 10.1016/j.xcrm.2026.102827](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2026.102827)
- **What it adds:** Phase 2 basket trial of pembrolizumab in 154 patients with advanced rare cancers (ORR 14.8%, CBR 26.8%) confirms that pre-existing CD3+/CD8+ T cell infiltration, active T cell signaling in the TME, and the capacity of intermediate-infiltration tumors to recruit additional immune cells on treatment predict clinical benefit. TMB and MSI-H status partially but incompletely explain this advantage. Consistent with the established TME exclusion paradigm — no text change to synthesis documents.
