# Changelog — 2026-05-29

Four novel emerging findings added; one established finding refined.

## Novel

### Cancer-induced nerve injury promotes anti-PD-1 resistance (TME exclusion)

- **Classification:** novel
- **Paper:** Baruch EN et al. "Cancer-induced nerve injury promotes resistance to anti-PD-1 therapy." *Nature*, August 2025. [PMID: 40836096](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40836096/)
- **What it adds:** Perineural invasion (PNI) and cancer-induced nerve injury (CINI) reshape the TME via IL-6/IFN-β neuronal signalling, promoting anti-PD-1 resistance. PNI/CINI burden correlates with worse ICI outcomes in patients; anti-IL-6R (tocilizumab) rescues anti-PD-1 sensitivity in nerve-injured tumour models. Connects peripheral nervous system remodelling — a common but understudied cancer hallmark — to immune evasion and T-cell exclusion. New bullet added to `docs/index.md` (emerging) and `docs/mechanisms/tme-exclusion.md` (Emerging).

### TMEM87A maintains Golgi pH to suppress ferroptosis and confer ICI resistance (tumor-intrinsic)

- **Classification:** novel
- **Paper:** Li et al. "TMEM87A suppresses ferroptosis and increases cancer immunotherapy resistance by maintaining the Golgi apparatus pH homeostasis." *Nature Cancer*, April 2026. [DOI: 10.1038/s43018-026-01156-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43018-026-01156-9)
- **What it adds:** TMEM87A maintains Golgi apparatus pH homeostasis to suppress ferroptosis, conferring resistance to PD-1 blockade. TMEM87A ablation sensitizes tumours to anti-PD-1 in preclinical models; high TMEM87A IHC expression correlates with worse ICI outcomes in patients. Extends the ferroptosis–ICI connection from IFN-γ/lipid peroxidation to organelle-level pH control. New bullet added to `docs/index.md` (emerging) and `docs/mechanisms/tumor-intrinsic.md` (Emerging).

### SpaceCat spatial multiplex imaging defines TNBC ICI response predictors in TONIC trial (TME exclusion)

- **Classification:** novel
- **Paper:** Greenwald NF, Nederlof I et al. "Temporal and spatial composition of the tumor microenvironment predicts response to immune checkpoint inhibition in metastatic triple-negative breast cancer." *Nature Cancer*, February 2026. [PMID: 39975273](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39975273/)
- **What it adds:** SpaceCat spatial multiplex imaging of 103 TNBC patients in the TONIC phase 2 trial shows that spatial features — diversity score and T-cell border infiltration — outperform bulk PD-L1 IHC for predicting ICI response. Spatial context of immune cell positioning, not type abundance alone, predicts outcome. The publicly available SpaceCat framework enables systematic spatial TME classification. New bullet added to `docs/index.md` (emerging) and `docs/mechanisms/tme-exclusion.md` (Emerging).

### GIV/Girdin endocytic adaptor controls macrophage PD-1 surface availability (TME exclusion)

- **Classification:** novel
- **Paper:** Mullick et al. "An Endocytic Checkpoint Controls Macrophage PD-1." *bioRxiv*, April 2026. [DOI: 10.64898/2026.04.14.718292](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.04.14.718292)
- **What it adds:** GIV/Girdin, an endocytic adaptor, controls PD-1 surface availability on tumour macrophages; GIV loss causes hyperprogressive disease under anti-PD-1. Psychotropic drugs that disrupt PD-1 endocytosis phenocopy GIV loss. Defines a macrophage-intrinsic endocytic checkpoint as an unexpected resistance axis distinct from tumour-cell PD-L1 regulation. New bullet added to `docs/index.md` (emerging) and `docs/mechanisms/tme-exclusion.md` (Emerging).

## Refined

### JAK1/JAK2 and B2M LOF established bullet: CD40 agonist bypass via myeloid reprogramming

- **Classification:** refines
- **Paper:** Zimmerman MP, Huang AC, Cox EK, Al Abosy R et al. "Harnessing Inflammatory Monocytes to Overcome Resistance to Anti-PD-1 Immunotherapy." *bioRxiv / PMC*, February 2026. [DOI: 10.64898/2026.02.05.704029](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.02.05.704029)
- **What it adds:** CD40 agonism (anti-CD40 mAb) can bypass B2M/JAK-LOF antigen-presentation resistance through myeloid reprogramming, achieving antitumour immune responses without cognate T cell–antigen recognition. Refines the established JAK1/JAK2/B2M LOF bullet in `docs/index.md` and `docs/mechanisms/antigen-presentation.md` with a trailing clause noting this rescue pathway.
