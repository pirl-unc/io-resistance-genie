## Novel findings

### 1. Mesenchymal GBM transcriptional state as ICB resistance biomarker

**Classification:** novel  
**Paper:** Ghannam JY et al. "Tumor transcriptional state predicts survival in immune-checkpoint-blockade-treated glioblastoma." *Nature Cancer* (2026). DOI: [10.1038/s43018-026-01179-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43018-026-01179-2)

In 181 ICB-treated GBM patients profiled by bulk DNA/RNA sequencing and single-nucleus RNA-seq, the mesenchymal (MES) transcriptional subtype predicts improved OS on ICB (but not standard chemoradiation), associated with high HLA-I expression and greater T cell infiltration. Transition from MES to non-mesenchymal state is the molecular correlate of acquired ICB resistance.

Paradoxically, MES GBM — generally considered more aggressive — is the ICB-responsive subtype; the finding is explained by unexpectedly higher antigen presentation capacity in this state. Added to the "Emerging" section of [TME exclusion](../mechanisms/tme-exclusion.md).

### 2. Intrathecal nivolumab safe in leptomeningeal disease (IT-PD1/NOA-26 phase 1)

**Classification:** novel  
**Paper:** Wick W et al. "Intrathecal nivolumab in metastatic solid tumors with leptomeningeal disease: dose escalation part of the multicenter IT-PD1/NOA-26 phase 1 trial." *Nature Cancer* (2026). DOI: [10.1038/s43018-026-01185-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43018-026-01185-4)

Intrathecal (intraventricular) nivolumab dose escalation through 20, 30, 40, and 50 mg in 30 patients with leptomeningeal metastatic disease from solid tumors: 1 DLT at 40 mg in 18 per-protocol evaluable patients; dose escalation completed; dose expansion at 50 mg ongoing. Establishes that blood-brain barrier pharmacokinetics is a tractable resistance barrier in LMD. Added to the "Emerging" section of [Clinical interventions](../mechanisms/clinical-interventions.md).
