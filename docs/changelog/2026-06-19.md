## Novel findings

### Ago2 as a post-transcriptional suppressor of IFN-γ signaling and ICI resistance in NSCLC

- **Classification:** novel
- **Paper:** Argonaute 2 drives resistance to immune checkpoint inhibitors in immunorefractory non-small cell lung cancer. PLOS Biology, June 18, 2026. [DOI: 10.1371/journal.pbio.3003860](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.3003860)
- **What it adds:** Tumor cell-intrinsic Ago2 suppresses STAT1-mediated IFN-γ signaling via post-transcriptional regulation, creating an immunorefractory phenotype that drives ICI resistance in NSCLC. Genetic Ago2 deletion in syngeneic LLC tumors restores IFN signaling, upregulates CCL2 and CXCL10, promotes immune infiltration, and sensitizes tumors to ICI (3.5-fold growth reduction at day 18). High Ago2 combined with a low IFN gene signature correlates with worse survival in NSCLC patients treated with ICI. This finding repositions microRNA RISC machinery — previously studied as an oncogene amplifier — as a direct suppressor of tumor immunogenicity, adding a mechanistically novel layer to the IFN-γ resistance axis distinct from canonical JAK1/2/STAT1 genetic LOF.
