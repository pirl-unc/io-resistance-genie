## Novel

### BETi reinvigoration of exhausted T cells via MYC-ODC1 polyamine axis (novel)

- **Classification**: novel
- **Paper**: "Epigenetic reprogramming of T cell metabolism restores function and enhances anti-tumor immunity in lung cancer" — Nature Immunology, June 2026. [[Link]](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41590-026-02515-5)
- BET bromodomain inhibitors (BETis) reinvigorate exhausted CD8+ T cells from lung cancer malignant pleural effusions by activating the MYC-ODC1 polyamine biosynthesis pathway; ODC1 inhibition completely abolishes the effect. The mechanism is metabolic rather than direct epigenetic reversal of TOX or NFAT, identifying polyamine biosynthesis as a new axis for T cell reinvigoration orthogonal to existing checkpoint strategies.
