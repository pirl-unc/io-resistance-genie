### COMPASS: pan-cancer AI ICI response predictor
- **Paper:** Shen et al. 2026, *Nature Medicine* (2026-07-03). DOI: [10.1038/s41591-026-04502-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-026-04502-7) · PMID: 40385399
- **Finding:** COMPASS, a pan-cancer concept bottleneck transformer encoding bulk tumor RNA-seq through 44 biologically grounded immune concepts, predicts ICI response and generalizes across cancer types and treatment regimens. Provides interpretable predictions — each concept maps to a TME feature — validated across multiple independent cohorts.
- **Triage:** relevance 4/5, novelty 4/5

### PID1–oxysterol TAM fate switch
- **Paper:** Zheng et al. 2026, *Nature Cancer* (2026-06-16). DOI: [10.1038/s43018-026-01189-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43018-026-01189-0) · PMID: 42303776
- **Finding:** PID1 (phosphotyrosine interaction domain-containing protein 1) maintains immunosuppressive tumor-associated macrophage identity by limiting LDL receptor expression and oxysterol production. PID1 deficiency in myeloid cells generates oxysterols that switch macrophage fate to antitumoral phenotypes, improving anti-PD-1 efficacy. Pan-cancer human TAM data confirm elevated PID1 with immunosuppressive gene signatures; mouse genetic loss-of-function establishes causality.
- **Triage:** relevance 5/5, novelty 5/5
- **Added to:** [index.md](../index.md), [tme-exclusion.md](../mechanisms/tme-exclusion.md), [metabolic.md](../mechanisms/metabolic.md)

### mKRAS-VAX + dual checkpoint in MSS CRC phase I
- **Paper:** Wang et al. 2026, *Nature Communications* (~2026-06-25). DOI: [10.1038/s41467-026-74711-8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-74711-8)
- **Finding:** Phase I trial (NCT04117087) of mKRAS-VAX (pooled 6-mutation KRAS peptide vaccine: G12D/V/R/C/A, G13D) with nivolumab + ipilimumab in 13 patients with pretreated metastatic MMRp/MSS CRC. Both primary endpoints (safety and immunogenicity) met; 8/12 evaluable patients showed KRAS-specific T cell responses. Proof-of-concept that shared neoantigen vaccination can generate de novo anti-tumor T cell responses in an indication uniformly refractory to PD-1 monotherapy.
- **Triage:** relevance 4/5, novelty 4/5
