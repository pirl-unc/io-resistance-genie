# Changelog — 2026-07-31

## Novel findings added this run

### 1. TLS plasma cells generate citrullinated-protein antibodies after PD-1 blockade in NSCLC (novel)

- **Classification:** novel — antigen_presentation
- **Paper:** 2026 Cancer Cell — "Tumor-infiltrating plasma cell profiling after PD-1 blockade reveals tumor-specific antibodies"  
  DOI: [10.1016/j.ccell.2026.07.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ccell.2026.07.001)
- **What it adds:** In NSCLC patients on neoadjuvant PD-1 blockade, TLS-associated CD138+ plasma cells generate antibodies specifically targeting citrullinated proteins — with citrullinated vimentin as the dominant antigen. PADI2 ablation eliminates this tumor reactivity, establishing citrullination as the essential post-translational modification step. A CAR-T using the PC-1 antibody from this profiling targets both tumor cells and tumor-promoting myeloid cells in vivo without off-target toxicity. This extends TLS–B cell biology from a structural correlate of ICI response to a mechanistic source of functionally validated humoral antitumor targeting, identifying a previously uncharacterized tumor antigen class (citrullinated proteins) in immunotherapy responders.

### 2. PROTEXI DC vaccine repurposes SARS-CoV-2 CD4 memory to amplify antitumor CD8 responses (novel)

- **Classification:** novel — clinical_intervention
- **Paper:** Kang et al. 2026 Nature Communications — "The Dendritic Cell-based Vaccine PROTEXI leverages Antiviral CD4 T cell Memory to boost anti-tumor immune responses in mice"  
  DOI: [10.1038/s41467-026-74891-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-74891-3)
- **What it adds:** PROTEXI incorporates SARS-CoV-2 Spike helper epitopes into a DC-based vaccine, repurposing pandemic-acquired CD4+ T cell memory — present in virtually the entire global adult population — as a universal pre-formed helper signal. This bypasses the CD4 help deficiency that limits conventional cancer vaccines, amplifying tumor-specific CD8+ cytotoxic T cell responses in melanoma and breast cancer mouse models, with further enhancement by checkpoint immunotherapy combination. Introduces a population-scale vaccine design principle made newly feasible by the COVID-19 pandemic; clinical validation awaited.
