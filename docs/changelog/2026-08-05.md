## Novel findings

### TRAILshort: tumor-secreted TCR signaling disruptor (Jalali et al. 2026, JCI)

**DOI**: [10.1172/jci194449](https://doi.org/10.1172/jci194449)  
**Mechanism class**: tumor_intrinsic_signaling  
**Finding category**: novel

TRAILshort, a dominant-negative TRAIL splice variant expressed by cancer cells, binds DR5 on T cells and recruits SHP-1 phosphatase, causing ZAP-70 dephosphorylation and disruption of TCR signaling — thereby impairing T cell recognition of tumor cells and blocking CAR-T cell activity in humanized mouse models. This function (direct TCR signal disruption) is entirely separate from TRAILshort's known role as an inhibitor of TRAIL-induced apoptosis. TRAILshort RNA is elevated in human neoplasms, viral infections, and autoimmune diseases. Blocking TRAILshort restores T cell immunity in preclinical models.

**Why surprising**: TRAILshort was known only as an apoptosis inhibitor; this establishes it as a tumor-secreted immunosuppressant that operates via a novel DR5→SHP-1→ZAP-70 axis — mechanistically orthogonal to every checkpoint pathway (PD-1/PD-L1, CTLA-4, LAG-3, TIM-3) and resistance mechanism currently in the synthesis.
