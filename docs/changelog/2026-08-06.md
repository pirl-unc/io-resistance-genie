## Novel

### CCR7+ actDCs sustain intratumoral CTL activity (antigen_presentation)

**Koufaki, Zelenay et al. 2026, Immunity.** DOI: [10.1016/j.immuni.2026.07.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.immuni.2026.07.002)

First-of-kind genetic actDC depletion mouse models demonstrate that CCR7+ activated dendritic cells are required not only for naive CTL priming in tumor-draining lymph nodes but also for sustaining pre-activated CTLs within the tumor itself. Both cDC1- and cDC2-derived actDCs contribute via cross-presentation and cross-dressing respectively, in a cancer cell-type-dependent manner. actDC depletion abrogates both spontaneous and ICI-driven anti-tumor immunity.

**Why novel:** Prior dogma held that DCs prime T cells in lymph nodes and T cells then function autonomously in tumors. This reveals an ongoing actDC requirement within the tumor — a distinct failure mode for ICI therapy independent of T cell-intrinsic exhaustion. Not previously represented in the synthesis; mechanistically complementary to (not duplicative of) the TGF-β/CARM1 epigenetic suppression of cDC1 cross-presentation.

**Evidence tier:** emerging (mouse-only, single group, awaiting independent replication and human validation)
