## Novel findings

### PPARα/FILM/GSDME axis overcomes anti-PD-1 resistance in HCC

**Classification:** novel  
**Paper:** [Chen, Xiong, Huang et al. 2026, Nature Communications](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-75770-7)  
**Paper ID:** `10.1038/s41467-026-75770-7`

A fibroinflammatory liver microenvironment (FILM) suppresses GSDME-dependent pyroptosis in HCC tumor cells and drives immune exclusion, conferring primary and acquired anti-PD-1 resistance. Pharmacological PPARα activation reprograms FILM, restores GSDME-mediated pyroptosis, and synergizes with anti-PD-1 to reduce tumor burden in syngeneic mouse models and patient-derived organoids.

This is a mechanistically distinct resistance axis not previously represented in the synthesis: it connects hepatic fibroinflammatory stroma (a common comorbidity in HCC arising on NASH/MASLD backgrounds) to a specific cell-death suppression mechanism (GSDME pyroptosis) that gates immune visibility. PPARα fibrates are already approved drugs in metabolic liver disease, lowering the translational barrier for combination trials.
