## Novel findings (1)

### SSBP4 — tumor-intrinsic cholesteryl ester biosynthesis suppresses CD8+ T cells

- **Classification:** novel
- **Paper:** Ou et al. 2026 — "Cancer Cell–Intrinsic SSBP4 Enables Tumor Immune Evasion by Promoting Cholesterol Biosynthesis." *Cancer Immunology Research* 14(8):1216–1232. [https://doi.org/10.1158/2326-6066.CIR-25-1312](https://doi.org/10.1158/2326-6066.CIR-25-1312)
- **What it adds:** SSBP4, a previously uncharacterized protein, upregulates cholesterol synthesis genes in tumor cells, driving excess cholesteryl ester accumulation that directly impairs intratumoral CD8+ T-cell activation; SSBP4 ablation restores T-cell function and significantly improves anti-PD-1 efficacy in preclinical tumor models. This identifies cholesteryl ester (not free cholesterol) as the functionally immunosuppressive lipid species, adding a new tumor-intrinsic metabolic axis to the resistance landscape distinct from T-cell-intrinsic cholesterol accumulation described in the exhaustion literature.
