## Novel findings added

### 1. UEIS micropeptide suppresses cGAS-STING in TAMs via TBK1 condensation
- **Classification:** novel
- **Paper:** Nature Cancer 2026, [DOI: 10.1038/s43018-026-01195-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43018-026-01195-2) (PMID 42426285)
- **What it adds:** USP30-AS1-encoded micropeptide UEIS sequesters TBK1 in biomolecular condensates within tumor-associated macrophages, suppressing cGAS-STING-mediated type I IFN signaling. Disrupting UEIS-TBK1 condensates enhances ICB efficacy. First lncRNA-encoded micropeptide acting as a phase-separation scaffold to suppress innate immunity in TAMs — expands the functional repertoire of the non-coding transcriptome in ICI resistance.

### 2. TAM ALDH2 silences CXCL9 via metabolic-epigenetic cascade
- **Classification:** novel
- **Paper:** Nature Communications 2026, [DOI: 10.1038/s41467-026-73234-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-73234-6) (PMID 42168200)
- **What it adds:** TAM-expressed ALDH2 generates 4-HNE which activates PI3K-AKT-EZH2 to deposit H3K27me3 at the CXCL9 promoter, silencing CXCL9 and blocking CD8+ T cell chemotaxis. Myeloid-conditional ALDH2 ablation restores CXCL9 and rescues anti-PD1 response. Mechanistically distinct from all existing macrophage-reprogramming strategies (CysLTR1, PID1, TREM2).

### 3. TME iron drives ACSL4-dependent ferroptosis in CAR-T cells
- **Classification:** novel
- **Paper:** Nature Cancer 2026, [DOI: 10.1038/s43018-026-01187-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43018-026-01187-2) (PMID 42270901)
- **What it adds:** Elevated iron in solid tumor TMEs promotes ACSL4-dependent ferroptotic death in CAR-T cells; ACSL4 ablation improves persistence and efficacy. Extends ACSL4 ferroptosis biology to T cell-intrinsic vulnerability driven by iron accumulation, complementing the immune-STING→ACSL4 lactylation axis already in the synthesis.

### 4. Tumor-intrinsic ADAR1 suppresses innate sensing and cDC1 activation in pancreatic cancer
- **Classification:** novel
- **Paper:** Cancer Immunology Research 2026, [DOI: 10.1158/2326-6066.CIR-25-1626](https://doi.org/10.1158/2326-6066.CIR-25-1626) (PMID 42308348)
- **What it adds:** ADAR1 edits dsRNA in pancreatic cancer cells to suppress innate immune sensing; ADAR1 deficiency triggers IFNβ→cDC1 activation→CD8+ T cell recruitment, sensitizing cold pancreatic tumors to anti-PD1. Positions RNA editing alongside B2M/JAK LOF and Ago2 as tumor-intrinsic programs suppressing immune recognition — but uniquely converts dsRNA accumulation into a cDC1-activating danger signal.

### 5. NDUFA9/complex I governs NK cell metabolic fitness in GBM
- **Classification:** novel
- **Paper:** Cancer Discovery 2026, [DOI: 10.1158/2159-8290.CD-25-0643](https://doi.org/10.1158/2159-8290.CD-25-0643) (PMID 41944586)
- **What it adds:** NDUFA9 deficiency impairs NK cell OXPHOS and cytotoxicity in GBM; restoring NDUFA9 or engineering NK cells with enhanced complex I activity improves antitumor efficacy. Extends metabolic resistance biology to NK cells and complex I in GBM, a tumor type where ICB has consistently failed.

### 6. ERO1A suppresses CCL4-driven CD8+ T cell recruitment in liver cancer
- **Classification:** novel
- **Paper:** Cancer Immunology Research 2026, [DOI: 10.1158/2326-6066.CIR-25-1471](https://doi.org/10.1158/2326-6066.CIR-25-1471) (PMID 42644613)
- **What it adds:** ERO1A in HCC suppresses CCL4 secretion and CD8+ T cell recruitment; ERO1A inhibition activates PERK/EIF2α/ATF4/CHOP, downregulates ADORA1, restores CCL4-driven infiltration, and synergizes with PD-L1 blockade in HCC preclinical models. Identifies ER oxidoreductase homeostasis as a parallel TME exclusion mechanism in liver cancer, distinct from the PPARα/fibroinflammatory-GSDME pathway already in the synthesis.
