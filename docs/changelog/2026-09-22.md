## 1 novel finding

### Clinical intervention

**VERSATILE-002 phase 2 — HPV16-targeted neoantigen vaccine + pembrolizumab in R/M HNSCC**
- **Paper:** Weiss J et al. 2026, JAMA Oncology ([doi:10.1001/jamaoncol.2026.3492](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaoncol.2026.3492))
- **Finding:** PDS0101 (Versamune HPV: lipid nanoparticle delivering HPV16 E6/E7 peptides) + pembrolizumab achieves mOS 39.3 months (95% CI 23.9–NE) in 53 ICI-naive HPV16+ R/M HNSCC patients with CPS ≥1; mOS 29.5 months (95% CI 15.3–NE) in the CPS 1-19 low PD-L1 subgroup; ORR 36%; grade ≥3 TRAEs 13.8%.
- **Why novel:** No prior entry in the synthesis covers HPV16-targeted T cell vaccination + PD-1 blockade in HNSCC. The 39.3-month mOS is ~3× the historical pembrolizumab monotherapy benchmark (~13–20 months in 1L CPS ≥1 R/M HNSCC); the 29.5-month mOS in the CPS 1-19 subgroup is especially striking, as this population historically derives limited anti-PD-1 benefit. This is the first phase 2 evidence that antigen-specific T cell priming via a tumor-associated viral antigen vaccine can overcome primary ICI resistance in a virally-driven solid malignancy.
