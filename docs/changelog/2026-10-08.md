### GSK3β–fumarate–neutrophil axis in CRC ICI resistance (tme_exclusion / metabolic)

**Paper:** He, Yang, Wu et al. 2026, *Nature Cancer* — "Intestinal epithelial GSK3β governs fumarate-dependent neutrophil reprogramming to promote colorectal cancer"  
**DOI:** [10.1038/s43018-026-01253-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43018-026-01253-9)

Tumor-cell GSK3β phosphorylates H1FX to increase glycolysis and fumarate production. Tumor-derived fumarate is secreted into the TME and reprograms neutrophils into an immunosuppressive state via H3K36 dimethylation, suppressing anti-tumor immunity and conferring anti-PD-1 resistance in colorectal cancer. Pharmacological GSK3β inhibition with tideglusib (a clinical-stage compound) combined with anti-PD-1 restores anti-tumor immunity and improves efficacy in preclinical CRC models.

**Why novel:** Fumarate has previously been studied as an intracellular SDH-mutant epigenetic silencing metabolite inside tumor cells; this study establishes a paracrine immunosuppressive role for tumor-derived extracellular fumarate acting on neutrophils — a cell type less studied than macrophages in ICI resistance. The mechanistic chain (GSK3β→H1FX→glycolysis→fumarate→H3K36me2 in neutrophils) is mechanistically orthogonal to CysLTR1-myelopoiesis, PID1-oxysterol, TREM2, and ALDH2/CXCL9-silencing strategies already in the synthesis.

### Tumor-intrinsic SIRPα drives anti-PD-1 resistance in ESCC (tumor_intrinsic_signaling)

**Paper:** 2026, *BMC Medicine* — "Tumor-intrinsic SIRPα drives anti-PD-1 resistance in esophageal cancer by coordinating proliferative dominance and T-cell exhaustion: A target for combinatorial immunotherapy"  
**DOI:** [10.1186/s12916-026-05213-z](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12916-026-05213-z)

Tumor-intrinsic SIRPα, transcriptionally activated by the YY1 transcription factor, drives anti-PD-1 resistance in esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (ESCC) via two parallel mechanisms: (1) YY1-SIRPα-PI3K/AKT signaling suppresses caspase-dependent apoptosis, and (2) SIRPα promotes T-cell exhaustion via a CD47-independent mechanism. Validated in 322 ESCC tumor samples, 161 paracancerous tissues, and a 21-sample scRNA-seq cohort. AAV9-delivered shSIRPα combined with pembrolizumab demonstrates therapeutic efficacy in humanized mouse models.

**Why novel:** SIRPα is canonically studied as a myeloid surface receptor mediating "don't eat me" signaling via CD47 on phagocytic cells. Tumor-intrinsic SIRPα expression as a YY1-driven PI3K/AKT survival and T-cell-exhaustion driver is a new axis entirely outside canonical myeloid checkpoint biology. The CD47-independence of the T-cell exhaustion arm distinguishes this from anti-CD47/SIRPα phagocytosis-based strategies already in clinical development. The large ESCC patient cohort provides good translational anchor for ESCC, a tumor type with historically limited ICI response.
