# Changelog — 2026-11-15

## Novel findings added

### 1. GDF-15 blockade with visugromab in neoadjuvant MIBC (GDFATHER-NEO phase 2a)
- **Classification:** novel
- **Paper:** Necchi A et al. "Neoadjuvant nivolumab with or without visugromab in muscle-invasive bladder cancer: the phase 2a GDFATHER-NEO trial." *Nature Medicine* (2026). [https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-026-04740-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-026-04740-9)
- **Summary:** Phase 2a randomized trial (n=31; NCT06059547) showing visugromab (anti-GDF-15 antibody) plus nivolumab achieves pCR 33% versus 8% over nivolumab monotherapy in neoadjuvant muscle-invasive bladder cancer. ORR 60% versus 15%; MPR 60% versus 23%.
- **What it adds:** GDF-15 is a stress-cytokine that blocks LFA-1-dependent T-cell extravasation into tumors. No prior entry in the synthesis described GDF-15 as a pharmacologically tractable T-cell exclusion driver. This is the first randomized clinical trial demonstrating that anti-GDF-15 antibody reverses T-cell exclusion and improves pathologic response with anti-PD-1. Added as an `emerging` bullet in both `docs/index.md` (§ New directions worth watching) and `docs/mechanisms/tme-exclusion.md` (§ Emerging). Human-study evidence table updated in both pages.
