# Mechanisms of resistance to immune checkpoint inhibitors

A living literature review of ICI resistance across PD-1, PD-L1, CTLA-4, LAG-3, TIGIT, TIM-3, and combination regimens. Regenerated on each scheduled update — see [changelog](changelog/2026-06-29.md).

<p class="tier-legend">Supporting evidence: <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> patient, trial, or primary tissue &nbsp;·&nbsp; <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> in-vivo mouse work &nbsp;·&nbsp; <span class="sp sp-other">other animal</span> non-mouse, non-human in vivo &nbsp;·&nbsp; <span class="sp sp-invitro">in vitro</span> cell-line / primary-cell / organoid</p>

## What has held up

- <span class="tier tier-est">established</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **Pre-existing CD8+ T-cell infiltrate at the invasive margin predicts pembrolizumab response in melanoma** (Tumeh 2014)[^pmid:25428505]. The inflamed / excluded / desert taxonomy has been validated pan-tumor and is the backbone of most subsequent biomarker frameworks (Chen & Mellman 2017)[^pmid:28102259].
- <span class="tier tier-est">established</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **TMB predicts anti-PD-1 benefit in NSCLC** (Rizvi 2015)[^pmid:25765070] and anti-CTLA-4 benefit in melanoma (Snyder 2014)[^pmid:25409260]. The association survives, but **see "contradictions" for the pan-tumor threshold debate**.
- <span class="tier tier-est">established</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **dMMR/MSI-H is the single most reliable ICI biomarker across histologies** (Le 2017)[^pmid:28596308]; basis of the first tissue-agnostic FDA approval. Confirmed in KEYNOTE-177 for 1L dMMR mCRC[^pmid:33264544] — but not uniformly predictive (see contradictions).
- <span class="tier tier-est">established</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> **JAK1/JAK2 and B2M LOF cause acquired anti-PD-1 resistance in melanoma** (Zaretsky 2016)[^pmid:27433843]; rare (<5%) but biochemically definitive. JAK1/2 LOF also drives a subset of primary resistance (Shin 2017)[^pmid:27903500]. PTPN2 loss in tumor cells is the inverse — a sensitizer that amplifies IFN-γ response (Manguso 2017)[^pmid:28723893], now in clinical development. CD40 agonism can bypass B2M/JAK-LOF antigen-presentation resistance through myeloid reprogramming without requiring cognate T cell–antigen recognition (Zimmerman 2026)[^doi:10.64898/2026.02.05.704029].
- <span class="tier tier-est">established</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **KRAS-mutant NSCLC with STK11/LKB1 co-mutation is primary ICI-refractory** despite TMB-intermediate/high status (Skoulidis 2018)[^pmid:29773717]. Now used in NSCLC trial stratification.
- <span class="tier tier-est">established</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **PD-1 + CTLA-4 combination rescues a meaningful fraction of single-agent PD-1 non-responders in melanoma**, with the largest relative benefit in PD-L1-negative tumors (CheckMate 067)[^pmid:26027431] — indicating CTLA-4 and PD-1 address partly-complementary resistance mechanisms. ~55% grade 3/4 TRAE cost.
- <span class="tier tier-est">established</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **PD-L1 IHC (TPS ≥50%) prospectively identifies the NSCLC sub-population for whom single-agent PD-1 blockade is sufficient** (KEYNOTE-024)[^pmid:27718847]. The PD-L1-low population defines the monotherapy resistance group; they now receive chemo-IO by default.
- <span class="tier tier-est">established</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> **Response depends on a stem-like Tcf1+PD-1+ CD8+ progenitor pool, not reinvigoration of terminally exhausted effectors** (Siddiqui 2019, Sade-Feldman 2018, Miller 2019)[^pmid:30635237][^pmid:30388456]. Explains why a heavily PD-1+ infiltrate is not enough if it lacks the stem-like subset.
- <span class="tier tier-est">established</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> **TOX is the master transcription factor of the exhausted chromatin state** (Alfei 2019 and concurrent work)[^pmid:31207603]. Epigenetic fixation of exhaustion is a durable barrier to reinvigoration — and TOX ablation impairs both exhaustion *and* persistence, so it is not a simple therapeutic brake.
- <span class="tier tier-est">established</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> **TGF-β signaling in peritumoral fibroblasts drives T-cell exclusion** (Mariathasan 2018 in urothelial[^pmid:29443960]; Tauriello 2018 in MSS CRC GEMM[^pmid:29443964]). **But clinical translation has failed — see contradictions.**
- <span class="tier tier-est">established</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **Canonical resistance taxonomy — primary / adaptive / acquired** (Sharma 2017)[^pmid:28187290] — remains the organizing framework.

## Where the field has contradicted itself (the surprises)

Known initial findings that later weakened under replication or didn't translate. Useful to avoid over-weighting any single-study narrative.

- <span class="tier tier-cont">contested</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **HLA-I LOH is a selected immune-escape event (~40% of NSCLCs, McGranahan 2017[^pmid:29107330]) but is *not* deterministic for ICI response.** Subsequent pan-cancer ICI cohorts show some HLA-LOH patients still respond. What matters is *which* allele is lost (loss of the allele presenting a dominant neoantigen), tumor type, and whether residual heterozygous HLA alleles can still present key neoantigens. So: "this patient has HLA LOH" does not imply "this patient will fail anti-PD-1."
- <span class="tier tier-cont">contested</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **β2M loss does not uniformly predict resistance either.** Documented case-level resistance (Zaretsky 2016[^pmid:27433843]) and rare B2M-low/null tumors that still respond suggest NK-mediated or non-classical HLA-dependent mechanisms can partially compensate. The acquired-resistance rate attributable to β2M/JAK is a small fraction of overall failures.
- <span class="tier tier-cont">contested</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> **Microbiome responder signatures do not replicate cleanly across geographies.** Gopalakrishnan 2018 (Faecalibacterium/Ruminococcaceae)[^pmid:29097493], Routy 2018 (Akkermansia)[^pmid:29097494], Matson 2018 (Bifidobacterium longum), and Sivan 2015[^pmid:26541606] / Vétizou 2015[^pmid:26541610] mouse work each nominated different beneficial taxa. Meta-analyses (Lee 2022, McCulloch 2022 Nat Med) found no single taxon reliably predicts anti-PD-1 response across cohorts. For *Akkermansia* specifically, Derosa 2022 Nat Med showed a non-monotonic relationship — very high levels were worse than intermediate. **Bacteroidales sign-flipped**: beneficial in Vétizou's anti-CTLA-4 mouse work, associated with non-response in Gopalakrishnan's anti-PD-1 cohort. The antibiotic-harm signal is the only microbiome finding that has replicated consistently. A melanoma-specific meta-analysis (Zhang 2026, n=678, 7 ICI cohorts)[^doi:10.1038/s43856-026-01612-8] found Faecalibacterium and SCFA biosynthesis to be the most reproducible cross-cohort features within that disease, suggesting disease-specific functional pathway analyses may outperform pan-cancer taxon abundance measures.
- <span class="tier tier-cont">contested</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **FMT rescues anti-PD-1-refractory melanoma** (Baruch 2021[^pmid:33303685]: 3/10; Davar 2021[^pmid:33542131]: 6/15 benefit including ORR ~20%). This was unexpected because refractoriness had been widely assumed to reflect tumor-intrinsic escape (B2M/JAK loss), not a reversible host-ecosystem state. The convergent independent replication at Sheba and NCI/Pittsburgh using different donors strengthens the signal.
- <span class="tier tier-cont">contested</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **TGF-β is a biologically robust exclusion driver that has clinically disappointed.** Bintrafusp alfa (TGFβRII trap / anti-PD-L1 bifunctional) failed pivotal trials — INTR@PID lung 037 discontinued 2021, biliary tract halted. Galunisertib + durvalumab, NIS793 combinations in MSS CRC — modest or negative. A reproducibility gap between GEMM and clinic; likely reflects redundancy, dose-limiting cardiotoxicity of TGF-β antagonism, or context-specific effects.
- <span class="tier tier-cont">contested</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **bTMB failed prospective validation.** Gandara 2018[^pmid:30082870] retrospectively showed bTMB-high enriches atezolizumab benefit in POPLAR/OAK; prospective B-F1RST did not confirm a first-line predictive effect. bTMB is not broadly adopted.
- <span class="tier tier-cont">contested</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **PBRM1 LOF as ICI sensitizer in ccRCC has not replicated.** Miao 2018[^pmid:29301960] reported enrichment in responders in two cohorts; subsequent CheckMate-009/010/025 (Braun 2020) and IMmotion analyses did not confirm.
- <span class="tier tier-cont">contested</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **IPRES transcriptomic signature has underperformed as an independent predictor.** Hugo 2016[^pmid:26997480] nominated an EMT/wound-healing / angiogenesis program in innately resistant melanoma. Validation has been mixed — IPRES overlaps heavily with TGF-β and stromal signatures and adds little above CD8, PD-L1, TMB, and pan-fibroblast measures.
- <span class="tier tier-cont">contested</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **dMMR is not a universal ICI predictor even in 1L mCRC.** KEYNOTE-177[^pmid:33264544] PFS was biphasic — pembrolizumab underperformed chemotherapy in the first ~6 months (~29% primary progression), and the final OS analysis did not reach statistical significance (HR ~0.74, crossover confounded).
- <span class="tier tier-cont">contested</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> **WNT/β-catenin activation as clinical exclusion driver is weaker than Spranger 2015[^pmid:25970248] mouse biology suggested.** Subsequent clinical cohorts show context-dependent correlations; WNT inhibition + ICI limited by toxicity.
- <span class="tier tier-cont">contested</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **The specific "shared neoantigen tetrapeptide signature" from Snyder 2014** did not replicate in larger cohorts; the TMB association survives but the signature itself is widely considered a small-sample artifact.

## Suspected but unconfirmed (mechanistically coherent, not yet clinically validated)

- <span class="tier tier-susp">suspected</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> <span class="sp sp-invitro">in vitro</span> **PD-1 partially *protects* clonally expanding T cells from restimulation-induced cell death**[^pmid:41748562]. In vitro human primary T cells only. If replicated in vivo, would complicate the "release the brakes" framing and explain some non-durability of response.
- <span class="tier tier-susp">suspected</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> **AARS1-mediated PD-L1 K280 lactylation stabilizes PD-L1 against HUWE1 ubiquitination**[^pmid:41864972]. Counterintuitively, exogenous lactate *enhances* anti-PD-L1 efficacy preclinically — this paradox must be reconciled before clinical translation.
- <span class="tier tier-susp">suspected</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> **HILPDA-driven lipogenesis palmitoylates PD-L1 at Cys272**[^pmid:41876831]. Fenretinide (TRIM21 engager) degrades HILPDA and restores anti-PD-1 efficacy in breast cancer models. Single tumor type; preclinical only; but fenretinide has existing clinical exposure.
- <span class="tier tier-susp">suspected</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **deltaHED (germline + somatic HLA-I evolutionary divergence) predicts worse PD-1 outcomes despite higher TMB/neoantigen load** across three cohorts[^pmid:41601354]. Inverts a naïve HED prediction; needs prospective single-agent PD-1 validation.
- <span class="tier tier-susp">suspected</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> **Hypoalbuminemia causally drives ICI resistance via macrophage arginine biosynthesis impairment** (LLC mice)[^pmid:41940988]. Would recast a routine prognostic lab as a reversible driver; dietary arginine rescue would need clinical study.
- <span class="tier tier-susp">suspected</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **Hyperprogression on anti-PD-1** (Champiat 2017, Kato 2017 class of reports) remains contested; definitions vary and natural-history confounding is substantial. Not listed as established.

## New directions worth watching (emerging this period)

- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **ATOMIC phase 3 (NEJM 2026): adjuvant atezolizumab + mFOLFOX6 in stage III dMMR colon cancer, HR 0.50**[^pmid:41880612]. 3-year DFS 86.3% vs 76.2%. Extends ICI benefit into adjuvant dMMR. OS not yet mature. A new large population will now experience prolonged adjuvant ICI exposure, creating a fresh substrate for late-resistance biology.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> **KLRG1 nominated as a novel inhibitory checkpoint** in anti-PD-1-resistant melanoma[^pmid:41956544]; novel anti-human KLRG1 mAb reduces tumor progression in humanized KI mice via combined CD8, NK, and γδ-T effects. Distinct from PD-1/CTLA-4/LAG-3/TIM-3.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **Anti-TIM-3 (TQB2618) + anti-PD-1 penpulimab achieves 52% ORR in PD-1-pretreated classical Hodgkin lymphoma** (n=21, phase Ib)[^pmid:41963080]. Salvage signal in a setting where re-engaging checkpoint biology was not expected to help.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> **TROP2-claudin-7 tight junctions functionally exclude T cells from TNBC**[^pmid:41932810] — reframes TROP2 from ADC target to barrier-mechanism target; mechanistic rationale for TROP2 ADC + anti-PD-1 combinations beyond cytotoxic payload delivery.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> **PKMYT1 inhibition (clinical-grade RP-6306) activates cGAS-STING in castration-resistant prostate cancer**[^pmid:41617394]. Converts cold CRPC to anti-PD-L1-responsive in preclinical models; prostate cancer has been stubbornly ICI-refractory.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> **IFN-γ → IRF1 → AGPAT3 axis sensitizes tumors to ferroptosis**[^pmid:41807033]. Extends IFN-γ consequences beyond antigen presentation and apoptosis to a lipidomic vulnerability.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **8-gene k-TSP + mucinous composite biomarker identifies a 15% dMMR/MSI-H mCRC subgroup with extreme benefit (HR 0.10) from anti-CTLA-4 addition to anti-PD-1**[^pmid:41950572]. Retrospective (n=25 in positive subgroup); prospective validation called for. If it holds, would resolve a decade-long open question.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **LOAd703 (CD40L/4-1BBL oncolytic adenovirus) + atezolizumab in anti-PD-1-refractory melanoma restores ICI-responsive immune signatures** in 24 patients[^pmid:41888981]. Biomarker-level evidence of myeloid-compartment rescue in a refractory population.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **Single-cell spatial profiling defines six niches stratifying neoadjuvant cSCC response better than PD-L1 IHC**[^pmid:41617396]. Niche-composition biomarkers may eventually outperform single-marker IHC.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **hMENA TGF-β-driven CAF signature validated against OAK phase III**[^pmid:41592891] — a clinically anchored exclusion biomarker despite the TGF-β clinical translation gap on the drug side.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> **TREM2+ immunosuppressive macrophages co-define CRC ICI resistance with multi-checkpoint T cell exhaustion (TIM3/LAG3/TIGIT/PD-1)**[^doi:10.1016/j.xcrm.2026.102786]. Quadruple blockade (PD-1 + CTLA-4 + LAG-3 + anti-TREM2) achieves 100% tumor clearance in dMMR CRC and >70% in MSS CRC orthotopic models; first evidence that myeloid-mediated spatial exclusion is co-tractable with conventional checkpoints in MSS CRC.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **Pan-cancer spatial ecotype (SE) taxonomy (9 conserved SEs from 10 M+ cells) recoverable non-invasively from plasma cfDNA** (Liquid EcoTyper) with striking ICI-response associations in ~100 melanoma patients[^doi:10.1038/s41586-026-10452-4]. First non-invasive reconstruction of tumor spatial TME state from blood; extends spatial transcriptomics from tissue biopsy to liquid biopsy.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> **STING agonists paradoxically expand immunosuppressive regulatory B cells (Bregs) via PI3Kγ in pancreatic cancer**, partially offsetting STING-driven myeloid activation[^doi:10.1038/s43018-026-01158-7]. Dual targeting with SH-273/Nano-273 (STING agonist + PI3Kγ inhibitor) suppresses Breg expansion while sustaining IRF3-driven myeloid activation, extending median survival to 201 days in orthotopic PDAC models — identifying Breg-mediated immune suppression as a co-resistance mechanism that explains partial failure of STING monotherapy.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> **KSR2 (kinase suppressor of Ras 2) acts as a tumor-intrinsic metabolic checkpoint for anti-PD-1 resistance in lung cancer**, driving Warburg-effect glucose reprogramming (enhanced uptake, lactate accumulation, TCA cycle disruption) that creates an immunosuppressive TME with reduced CD8+ TILs and enriched Tregs[^doi:10.1007/s00262-026-04394-z]. KSR2 knockdown resensitizes resistant tumors; links oncogenic Ras scaffold signaling to metabolic immune evasion as a druggable axis.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> **P2RX7 in tumor-infiltrating regulatory T cells (Tregs) senses extracellular ATP in the TME, promoting Treg accumulation and CTLA-4-mediated suppression in lung cancer**[^doi:10.1158/2326-6066.CIR-25-0567]. T cell-specific P2RX7 deletion reduces Treg infiltration, increases CD4+ effector T cells, and improves tumor control in mouse models; pharmacological inhibition phenocopies this effect; elevated P2RX7 correlates with worse survival in human lung cancer. Connects tumor-derived ATP — a danger signal from stressed and dying cells — to Treg persistence via a purinergic receptor; the mechanism is distinct from and potentially complementary to anti-CTLA-4 antibody depletion of Tregs.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> **CysLTR1 (the cysteinyl leukotriene receptor targeted by FDA-approved montelukast) programs tumor-promoting myelopoiesis that confers ICI resistance**[^doi:10.1038/s43018-026-01174-7]. Blocking CysLTR1 reprograms tumor-associated neutrophils and myeloid cells from immunosuppressive to immunostimulatory phenotypes — not depletion but re-education — slowing tumor growth, improving survival, and restoring anti-PD-1 efficacy even in tumors with acquired ICI resistance. Demonstrated across five syngeneic mouse cancer types (TNBC, melanoma, ovarian, colon, prostate) and validated in human immune cells and tumor tissues. Montelukast's established safety profile makes rapid clinical translation feasible.

- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> **Cancer-induced nerve injury (CINI) promotes anti-PD-1 resistance via IL-6/IFN-β neuronal signalling that remodels the TME**[^pmid:40836096]. Perineural invasion burden correlates with worse ICI outcomes in patients; anti-IL-6R (tocilizumab) rescues anti-PD-1 sensitivity in nerve-injured tumour models. Connects peripheral nervous system remodelling — a common but understudied cancer hallmark — to immune evasion.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **TMEM87A maintains Golgi apparatus pH homeostasis to suppress ferroptosis and confer resistance to PD-1 blockade**[^doi:10.1038/s43018-026-01156-9]. TMEM87A ablation sensitizes tumours to anti-PD-1 in preclinical models; high TMEM87A IHC expression correlates with worse ICI outcomes in patients. Extends the ferroptosis–ICI connection from IFN-γ/lipid peroxidation to organelle-level pH control.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **SpaceCat spatial multiplex imaging of 103 TNBC patients in the TONIC phase 2 trial reveals that spatial features — diversity score and T-cell border infiltration — outperform bulk PD-L1 IHC for predicting ICI response**[^pmid:39975273]. Spatial context of immune cell positioning, not abundance alone, predicts outcome; the publicly available SpaceCat framework enables systematic spatial TME classification across tumour types.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> **GIV/Girdin endocytic adaptor controls PD-1 surface availability on tumour macrophages; GIV loss causes hyperprogressive disease under anti-PD-1**[^doi:10.64898/2026.04.14.718292]. Psychotropic drugs that disrupt PD-1 endocytosis phenocopy GIV loss. Defines a macrophage-intrinsic endocytic checkpoint as an unexpected resistance axis, distinct from tumour-cell PD-L1 regulation.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **Mesenchymal (MES) GBM transcriptional subtype predicts improved survival on ICB but not standard chemoradiation in 181 patients** (Ghannam 2026)[^doi:10.1038/s43018-026-01179-2]. MES GBM is marked by high HLA-I expression and greater T cell infiltration at baseline; transition from MES to a non-mesenchymal state is the molecular correlate of acquired ICB resistance. Paradoxical finding — mesenchymal GBM, generally more aggressive, is the ICB-responsive subtype.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **Intrathecal nivolumab dose escalation (20-50 mg) is safe in leptomeningeal disease** (IT-PD1/NOA-26, n=30; Wick 2026)[^doi:10.1038/s43018-026-01185-4]. 1 DLT at 40 mg in 18 evaluable patients; dose expansion at 50 mg ongoing. First human proof-of-concept that CNS-compartment anti-PD-1 delivery is feasible, establishing that blood-brain barrier pharmacokinetics — not tumour-intrinsic biology alone — is a tractable resistance barrier in leptomeningeal disease.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **Ago2 (Argonaute 2) acts as a tumor-intrinsic post-transcriptional suppressor of STAT1-mediated IFN-γ signaling in NSCLC, driving an immunorefractory phenotype resistant to ICI**[^doi:10.1371/journal.pbio.3003860]. Genetic Ago2 deletion restores IFN-γ signaling, upregulates CCL2 and CXCL10, promotes myeloid and T cell infiltration, and converts ICI-resistant LLC tumors to ICI-sensitive (3.5-fold growth reduction with ICI at day 18). High Ago2 expression combined with a low IFN gene signature correlates with worse survival in NSCLC patients treated with ICI. Repositions microRNA machinery — previously studied as an oncogenic growth regulator — as a direct post-transcriptional brake on tumor immunogenicity; mechanistically distinct from canonical JAK/STAT genetic LOF resistance.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> <span class="sp sp-invitro">in vitro</span> **BET bromodomain inhibitors (BETis) reinvigorate exhausted CD8+ T cells from lung cancer malignant pleural effusions via the MYC-ODC1 polyamine biosynthesis axis**[^doi:10.1038/s41590-026-02515-5]. BETis activate MYC→ODC1 to expand intracellular polyamine pools and alter chromatin accessibility; scRNA-seq shows a shift from terminal toward progenitor-like exhausted T cells. ODC1 genetic or pharmacological inhibition abolishes the effect. Identifies polyamine metabolism — not direct epigenetic reversal of TOX/NFAT — as the proximate reinvigoration signal, mechanistically distinct from current exhaustion-reversal strategies.

- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> **Gut microbiome-driven restoration of MHC-II expression in colon cancer cells overcomes PD-1 resistance in MSS CRC**[^doi:10.64898/2026.06.30.735621]. Loss of epithelial MHC-II promotes immune evasion in intestinal tumors; microbial signals restore antigen presentation capacity, promote T cell recognition, and limit metastasis in preclinical models. Bridges the gut microbiome and antigen presentation biology in an indication where both axes are implicated but had not previously been mechanistically connected.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **Single-cell multi-omics reveals heterogeneous and plastic neutrophil states in HNSCC that govern ICI response**[^doi:10.64898/2026.07.02.735691]. Neutrophil-activating therapy (NAT: anti-CD40 + TNFα + Cetuximab) induces distinct anti-tumoral neutrophil states absent from cold tumors; ICAM1 expression marks ICI-responsive states and functions as an ICI response biomarker in HNSCC; elevated TAN density in cold tumors correlates with poor ICI prognosis. Extends myeloid-compartment heterogeneity as a determinant of checkpoint sensitivity to neutrophil state plasticity.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> **Immune cell-intrinsic STING activation drives paracrine tumor ferroptosis in CRC via an arachidonic acid → ACSL4 lactylation axis**[^doi:10.1073/pnas.2524594123]. TBK1 phosphorylates cPLA2 at Ser505 downstream of STING, releasing arachidonic acid (AA) into the TME; adjacent CRC cells take up AA, which inhibits EP300-mediated ACSL4 K426 lactylation and promotes ACSL4-dependent ferroptosis. STING agonist synergizes with anti-PD-1 by engaging this immune-to-tumor relay. Mechanistically orthogonal to the existing IFN-γ→IRF1→AGPAT3 ferroptosis axis — this is immune compartment-initiated and paracrine rather than autocrine tumor-cell-intrinsic.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> **Diet–microbiome synergy is the mechanistic basis for the obesity paradox in ICI response**[^doi:10.1038/s41586-026-10750-x]. Across 12 custom mouse diet models, obesity-associated ICI sensitivity correlates with diet-driven gut microbial ecosystem features rather than body weight or metabolic parameters per se; an obesogenic diet promotes a robust and persistent gut microbial ecosystem that enhances anti-PD-1 efficacy. Short-term diet switching or human-to-mouse FMT restores ICI sensitivity — decoupling the benefit from bodyweight itself and identifying dietary modification as a potential intervention complementary to FMT.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **COMPASS, a pan-cancer concept bottleneck transformer using 44 interpretable immune-concept features from bulk tumor RNA-seq, predicts ICI response and generalizes across cancer types and treatment regimens**[^doi:10.1038/s41591-026-04502-7]. Each of the 44 immune concepts encodes a biologically grounded immune cell state or TME interaction program, providing mechanistic interpretability absent from black-box deep learning biomarkers. Validated across multiple independent cohorts spanning different cancers and treatments; may bridge the gap between single-marker approaches (PD-L1, TMB) and spatial transcriptomics in pre-treatment ICI response triage.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **PID1 (phosphotyrosine interaction domain-containing protein 1) maintains immunosuppressive tumor-associated macrophage identity by limiting oxysterol production; PID1 loss in myeloid cells generates oxysterols that switch TAM fate to antitumoral phenotypes and improve anti-PD-1 efficacy**[^doi:10.1038/s43018-026-01189-0]. PID1 deficiency upregulates LDL receptor expression, driving oxysterol accumulation that reprograms macrophages away from immunosuppressive states; pan-cancer human TAM data confirm elevated PID1 with immunosuppressive gene signatures. A new oxysterol-mediated macrophage fate axis orthogonal to existing myeloid reprogramming strategies (CysLTR1, TREM2, CSF1R).
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **Mutant KRAS peptide vaccine (mKRAS-VAX) targeting six KRAS mutations (G12D/V/R/C/A, G13D) combined with nivolumab + ipilimumab meets safety and immunogenicity primary endpoints in 13 patients with pretreated metastatic MMRp/MSS CRC** (phase I)[^doi:10.1038/s41467-026-74711-8]. KRAS-specific T cell responses induced in 8/12 evaluable patients. Directly addresses the core ICI-resistance mechanism of MSS CRC — low tumor neoantigen burden — by supplying shared KRAS neoantigens; clinical activity signals in an indication where PD-1 monotherapy has uniformly failed.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **Tumor-cell P2RY2 senses immunotherapy-induced extracellular ATP to drive COX1/COX2-PGE2 immunosuppression — an adaptive resistance loop amplified by ICI itself**[^doi:10.1016/j.immuni.2026.06.006]. Dying tumor and immune cells release eATP during ICI-induced killing; P2RY2 on surviving tumor cells detects this danger signal to upregulate COX1/2 and accumulate intratumoral PGE2, re-establishing immunosuppression in a feedback circuit that worsens with each therapeutic response. Genetic deletion or pharmacological P2RY2 inhibition reduces baseline intratumoral PGE2, remodels the TME immune infiltrate, and restores T cell responses across CRC, pancreatic, and melanoma preclinical models; an anti-P2RY2 mAb enhanced melanoma patients' immune cells' ability to kill their own tumor cells ex vivo (Hu et al. 2026, Immunity). P2RY2 inhibition reverses resistance to CAR-T, TCR-T, checkpoint blockade, and TIL therapies simultaneously — one of few mechanisms offering pan-modality sensitization. Mechanistically distinct from COX2/PGE2 inhibition as a standalone strategy: P2RY2 acts upstream as the eATP sensor that initiates the cascade specifically in response to ICI-driven cell death.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> **Tumor-derived GABA activates GABBR on granulocytic MDSCs to suppress antitumor T cells via the CAT2–L-arginine–NOS2 axis in GBM, but exclusively in female mice**[^doi:10.1038/s43018-026-01183-6]. GABBR agonism promotes GBM growth in female preclinical models through gMDSC-mediated immunosuppression; pharmacological GABBR antagonism extends survival and reduces NOS2 in tumor-infiltrating gMDSCs in female but not male mice. A sex-specific neurotransmitter–myeloid immunosuppressive axis: a classical inhibitory neurotransmitter produced by GBM cells hijacks myeloid suppressive function in a manner that depends on biological sex. Has direct implications for sex-stratified ICI trial design in GBM, where female sex is associated with distinct TME immune composition (Pathak & Deneen 2026, Nature Cancer).
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **Citraconate (an itaconate isomer) is depleted from exhausted and hypoxic tumor-infiltrating CD8 T cells; its loss drives stemness collapse and ferroptosis via the PDE1A/C–cAMP–PKA–ALOX5 axis**[^doi:10.1126/sciimmunol.adz0348]. Citraconate supplementation preserves stem-like T cell characteristics, limits ALOX5-driven arachidonic acid peroxidation, maintains mitochondrial integrity, and potentiates ICI in preclinical models. Patients with cancer who have lower ALOX5 or PDE1A expression have less T cell exhaustion and better ICI responsiveness (Liu et al. 2026, Science Immunology). The mechanism is orthogonal to TOX/NFAT-mediated exhaustion programs, BETi/polyamine reinvigoration, and existing ferroptosis resistance axes (TMEM87A-Golgi pH, AGPAT3) — a distinct metabolite-depletion vulnerability in the exhausted T cell compartment with a patient-level biomarker pair (ALOX5, PDE1A) ready for prospective validation.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> **Tumor progression hijacks thymic central tolerance via CCR9-dependent pDC recruitment, deleting tumor-reactive T cells before they can populate the periphery**[^doi:10.1126/sciadv.adx9864]. Two distinct pDC populations accumulate in the thymus of tumor-bearing mice: CDP-derived pDCs migrate from the periphery, present tumor antigens via MHC-I/II, and drive clonal deletion of tumor-specific developing T cells; CLP-derived pDCs secrete IFN-α to suppress thymopoiesis and reduce naïve T cell output. CCR9 deficiency abrogates both populations, enhancing antitumor immunity and reducing tumor growth in syngeneic models. A systemic immune evasion axis entirely outside the TME — operating at the level of central T cell repertoire selection — and one that PD-1 blockade cannot rescue T cells that were never generated (Wang, Kato, Katakai 2026, Science Advances).
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> <span class="sp sp-invitro">in vitro</span> **Common TP53 mutations (G242V, S258I) in CRC selectively retain mTORC1-suppressive function despite losing canonical activities, driving autophagy-mediated resistance to IFN-γ/TNF-α apoptosis and anti-PD-1 therapy**[^doi:10.1038/s41419-026-09067-4]. CRISPR deletion of mutant Trp53 in MC38 CRC cells restores mTORC1, reduces autophagy, markedly increases CD8+ TIL infiltration and clonal expansion, and reduces Tregs; pharmacological mTOR activation or autophagy inhibition phenocopies sensitization to ICI. Human CRC cell lines confirm regulation of the same mTORC1 inhibitor gene sets. Two specific mutations characterized so far; generalizability to other p53 mutants and to MSS CRC (MC38 is MSI-H) requires validation. Inverts the expectation that TP53 LOF is purely a growth disinhibitor — these mutants actively confer immune evasion via autophagy, identifying a druggable vulnerability (mTOR/autophagy axis) for combination with ICI in the most common mutation in MSS CRC (Labani-Motlagh & Lu 2026, Cell Death & Disease).
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> <span class="sp sp-invitro">in vitro</span> **MEK-dependent bioenergetic demand drives terminal CD8+ T cell exhaustion via excessive protein synthesis, mitochondrial NADH accumulation, and reactive oxygen species production** (Mitra et al. 2026, Immunity)[^pmid:42442358]. MEK inhibition uniquely reduces nutrient uptake, reverses RNA Pol II CTD phosphorylation at exhaustion-associated loci while sparing memory gene transcription, and attenuates terminal exhaustion in vivo. Reframes terminal exhaustion progression as partly driven by an upstream bioenergetic overload — a metabolic demand mechanism upstream of the TOX/chromatin-fixation circuitry — and distinct from the polyamine (BETi/ODC1) and citraconate exhaustion-metabolic axes also recently identified. FDA-approved MEK inhibitors make near-term clinical testing feasible without new drug development.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> **CARM1 (coactivator-associated arginine methyltransferase 1) is a TGF-β-regulated epigenetic checkpoint that selectively suppresses cDC1 antigen cross-presentation in tumors** (Zhang, Wucherpfennig et al. 2026, Science)[^pmid:42424445]. CARM1 inactivation (genetic or pharmacological) promotes cDC1 accumulation, activation, and cross-presentation in the TME; a CARM1 inhibitor enhances cDC1-mediated T cell priming in the context of a neoantigen vaccine. TGF-β drives Carm1 expression, revealing an entirely separate, parallel mechanism by which TGF-β silences tumor immunity — DC-intrinsic epigenetic repression of antigen presentation, pharmacologically tractable independently of the stromal exclusion biology for which TGF-β is already known.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-human">human</span> **TLS-associated CD138+ plasma cells in NSCLC generate tumor-specific antibodies against citrullinated proteins after neoadjuvant PD-1 blockade; citrullinated vimentin is the dominant antigen and PADI2 ablation eliminates tumor reactivity**[^doi:10.1016/j.ccell.2026.07.001]. A CAR-T armed with the PC-1 antibody from this plasma cell profiling targets both tumor cells and tumor-promoting myeloid cells in vivo without off-target toxicity, identifying citrullination — a PTM central in RA autoimmunity — as a novel tumor antigen class in ICI responders and PADI2 as a druggable upstream regulator. Extends TLS–B cell biology from a structural ICI-response correlate to a mechanistic source of functionally validated humoral antitumor targeting.
- <span class="tier tier-emerg">emerging</span> <span class="sp sp-mouse">mouse</span> **PROTEXI, a DC-based vaccine incorporating SARS-CoV-2 Spike helper epitopes, repurposes pandemic-acquired CD4+ T cell memory as a universal pre-formed helper signal to amplify tumor-specific CD8+ cytotoxic responses in melanoma and breast cancer mouse models; efficacy is further enhanced by checkpoint immunotherapy combination**[^doi:10.1038/s41467-026-74891-3]. Bypasses the CD4-help deficiency that limits conventional cancer vaccines by exploiting SARS-CoV-2-specific memory present in virtually the entire global adult population — a population-scale vaccine design principle made newly feasible by the COVID-19 pandemic. Mouse preclinical; clinical validation awaited.

## Practical questions & quick answers

- **Is PD-L1 IHC still clinically useful?** Yes, for 1L NSCLC selection (TPS ≥50% standard for pembro monotherapy). Less useful elsewhere; increasingly replaced by tumor-type-specific algorithms.
- **Does dMMR status guarantee benefit?** No. KEYNOTE-177[^pmid:33264544] PFS was biphasic with ~29% primary progression. dMMR is necessary-but-not-sufficient in mCRC.
- **Does peri-ICI antibiotic exposure matter?** Yes — the antibiotic-harm signal is the most robustly replicated microbiome observation. Specific probiotic or taxonomy-guided interventions are not clinically ready.
- **Can anti-PD-1-refractory patients be rescued?** Emerging positive signals: FMT from a responder donor (~20–30% response in small trials), TIM-3 combination in PD-1-pretreated cHL (52% ORR n=21), LOAd703 oncolytic in melanoma (biomarker-level). None are standard of care yet.
- **Is STK11/KEAP1 testing informative in KRAS-mutant NSCLC?** Yes — it guides prognosis on ICI and supports clinical trial stratification, though no FDA-level companion biomarker yet.
- **Is there a prospective biomarker beyond PD-L1 / TMB / dMMR / STK11 ready for clinical use?** Not yet. HLA LOH, HED/deltaHED, microbiome, IPRES, PBRM1, bTMB — all research-only or have failed prospective validation.
- **What about hyperprogression?** Definitions vary; the literature is contested. Natural history and pseudoprogression confound the phenomenon. Not actionable today.
